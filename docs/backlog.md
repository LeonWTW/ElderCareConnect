## Problem Statement
The users of the product will be the elderly with declining cognitive function and relatives who want to be able to monitor them remotely and from anywhere in the world. The current problem now is that there's not much software or plausible solutions anywhere at the moment to do so, and this software aims to mitigate stress and concerns when no one is there to take care of those who need it.
