Cognitive game design research (Dementia Prevention)
    Date: 2/8/2026
    Tool: Gemini (Deep Research)
    Purpose: Study existing cognitive training platforms and open-source resources

Used Gemini to understand what already exists and what ideas could be reused.
I focused on:
    - Comparing major cognitive training platforms
    - Their strengths and limitations
    - Finding open-source cognitive game implementations
    - Practical design / system ideas

Existing Platforms Observed：

    1. BrainHQ – Strong research support, clinical-style training

    2. CogniFit – Structured tracking and cognitive assessment

    3. Lumosity – Strong gamification, but debated real cognitive impact

    4. Elevate – More educational than clinical

Open-Source References：
    1. jsPsych – Psychology experiment JS library (Stroop, visual search, etc.)

    2. Dual N-Back (web) – Working memory training logic

    3. MindMender – Example cognitive training web app

    4. Open-Pinball-Recall – Open-source spatial memory game

This helped me understand how real cognitive training systems are built and how we could borrow selected ideas while keeping our system simple and practical.
