Pinball Recall

A memory training game

How to Play

1
Memorize the positions and directions of the bumpers on the board. They will only be shown briefly!
2
The bumpers will disappear. A ball will enter from one side of the board.
3
Click on the edge where you think the ball will exit the board based on the bumper positions you memorized.
4
Watch the ball travel and see if you guessed correctly! Each correct guess earns you points.
